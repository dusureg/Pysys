## 4.6 - Contracting, labels, brands and pictures, invoices, video and 3D files, down payment, formal contract copy, company overview

Data formats are compatible: 4.6 only **adds** things (see [DATA-FORMAT.md](DATA-FORMAT.md#compatibility)). It is the second of the releases that answer the notes of 19 and 28 October (4.7 control: shifts, sessions, IT settings, costs into Finance; 4.8 speed and PC design; 4.9 Business Hub and company finance; then equipment, other storage, automation, offline work and AI).

- **Contracts per project and a Contracting section** (`#contracting`, new report `#reports/contracts`): one line per project with contract number, client, price, wording, language, status (**draft, issued, signed**) and dates, filters and search. The contract screen of a project now has its own **wording**: the standard clauses of Settings, a **named template** (Settings > Contract: up to 12 templates with English and Arabic clauses) or **clauses written for that one project** (copy the standard or a template wording as a start, edit, clear). Contract data stays private; nothing of it reaches the client site.
- **Formal copy in black and white** (checkbox on the contract screen): the same contract with black text, a black band instead of the brand colour, white table headers and grey hairlines, the logo in grey; it is a *view and print option* (the print title ends with `-bw`), not a second document to keep in step.
- **Labels** (`#labels/<client>/<project>`, also from the project): small stickers 2.5 x 2.5 cm or a strip 2.5 cm tall and any length, for the project or for **every piece**; choose what is printed (logo, a thin Egypt flag with the slogan, client and product data, the piece, a **QR code** and/or a **Code 128 barcode** of the SKU). Every label is a page of exactly its size (`@page lbl`), so a label printer or *Save as PDF* gives one label per page. The settings (size, length, slogan, fields, copies) are kept in Settings > Labels.
- **Brands and pictures of products**: a list of brands in Settings (up to 80); each inventory item points at a brand and can have a **picture** (shrunk on the device; a 72 px thumbnail is kept in the record and shown in the inventory list, the full picture is sealed in its own file).
- **Invoices and files on records**: expenses and shipments take **invoice files** (pictures are shrunk, PDFs are kept as they are, up to about 780 KB sealed each). Files are sealed with the master key (`data/private/files/<id>.bin`), added to a record in memory, uploaded when the record is saved and removed after it. They are never published.
- **Video and a better viewer on the client site**: a project can keep a **video** in the repository (sealed like a photo, with a poster; up to 24 MB) or a **video link**; a direct file (mp4, webm) plays inside the page, YouTube, Vimeo and other links open on their own site. The viewer zooms pictures (buttons, double tap, + - 0), moves with the arrow keys or a swipe, and the zoom buttons start disabled at 100 %.
- **Files to download for the client**: drawings and **2D / 3D files** (GLB, GLTF, OBJ, STL, STEP, SKP, 3DM, 3DS, FBX, DWG, DXF, PDF, ZIP, images, TXT, CSV, XLSX, DOCX, PPTX; up to 24 MB each) kept sealed per project and **saved** by the client, never opened by the page; the list is part of the client's private snapshot.
- **Save our contact** on the client site: a contact card (`.vcf`, vCard 3.0) built from the public company profile (name, phone, WhatsApp, e-mail, website, address), so the client can add the company to the phone's contacts.
- **Down payment**: one row of a collection schedule can be flagged *Down payment* (only one per project). The client site shows **Down payment** and **Next payment** boxes (the next payment is not shown twice when the next row is the down payment), the Collections screen and the Collections report mark the row.
- **Company overview report** (`#reports/overview`, button on the dashboard, first in the Reports list): the whole company on a few pages for the owner: clients, projects in production, contract value, collected, still to collect, overdue, late projects and stock alerts; status and money charts, what falls due by month, progress of running projects, the list of every project with totals and a **Needs attention** list (late instalments, late projects, empty and low stock, late shipments). Paper view, *Save as PDF*, Excel and picture like every other report; private.
- **Tests**: new suites `s29_files_brands`, `s30_media_viewer`, `s31_downloads_contact`, `s32_contracting`, `s33_labels` (the QR code is decoded from the printed PDF with a real decoder; the Code 128 bars are checked structurally and decoded independently), `s34_addons` (down payment, formal copy, overview), `s35_audit_46` (every new screen and the client viewer audited with the rules of s20). `run.py` now holds 1,313 checks (1,022 at 4.5), `boot.py` 24, `tests/tools/test_get_fonts.py` 24; the last full pass was 1,313 / 1,313.
- **Found while testing (all fixed)**: the viewer opened a small picture at about 1,440 px and had its zoom-out button live at 100 % (sizes now follow the fitted picture, buttons start disabled); *own wording* of a contract replaced the object the text boxes were bound to, so typing after *copy wording* or *clear* changed nothing (the clauses are now changed in place); the report row counts included the total row; a long label strip was squeezed to the width of the screen (the label area now scrolls, the label keeps its size); on a 2.5 cm label a Code 128 barcode of a whole SKU is too fine to scan, so the **QR code was added** and the barcode kept as an option with a hint; label settings were stale after a change in Settings. **Changing a client's key stopped with a misleading "Not found (404)" when one sealed file of the project was no longer in the repository** (the key change read every file first): a missing file is now skipped and the save says how many could not be re-encrypted. On a phone the project tabs of the Labels screen were 29 px tall (under the 40 px rule) and are 40 px now. The checks themselves were fixed three times, where they were at fault and not the product: a stock test read a stale toast, the "no plain text in the History" check searched 4-letter words in random base64 text (a chance match is possible), and two new suites left dialog handlers behind that disturbed later suites. **"Print report" on the Contracting screen kept the client picked in an earlier report**, so the Contracts report could show only that client (or nothing): the company-wide reports (Contracts, Overview) now start from all clients.
- **Not done on purpose / still open**: the Code 128 barcode was **not** read by a real scanner (no decoder library could be installed in the sandbox); the QR code was decoded with OpenCV from the printed PDF at 300 and 203 dpi. A real label printer, the phone's real share sheet and contact import, and real playback of YouTube / Vimeo were not available. There is **no 3D preview** in the browser: 3D files are downloaded and opened in the client's own program. Videos and files above 24 MB are refused (the contents API of GitHub and a phone's memory are the limits); larger ones belong on a drive, which is planned for 5.1. The first visit of the admin site now loads **84 files** (the budget was 80; the speed work in 4.8 is meant to bring it down again).

## 4.5 - Everyday tools: owner sign-in, profiles, import, share, report pictures, paper sizes, look settings, internal codes, collections

Data formats are compatible: 4.5 only **adds** things (see [DATA-FORMAT.md](DATA-FORMAT.md#compatibility)). It is the first of four releases that answer the notes of 19 October (4.6 contracts, labels and brands; 4.7 offline work, staff status, HR and call centre; 4.8 Gemini and client traffic).

- **Administrator sign-in without the GitHub token** (Team screen, *Administrator sign-in* card): choose a user name and a long password and open the address the card shows on any device (*User name* tab): the administrator signs in with those two, like a team member, with full rights. The admin password and token methods keep working as before. The login file is public like every file of a public repository, so the password must be long (the screen enforces 12 characters and says why); remove the sign-in if a device is lost.
- **Profile** (`#account`, for every signed-in person): name, phone and a picture (cropped and shrunk on the device to 192 px, at most 60 KB). Sealed in `data/private/profiles/<id>.json`, shown in the menu and the Team list; each person writes only their own file.
- **Import from CSV, Excel (.xlsx) or JSON** into **Inventory, Suppliers, Shipments and Expenses** (`#import/<section>`, also an *Import* button on each of those screens). Columns are matched by name in English or Arabic (semicolon or comma separated, Arabic digits understood), you can fix the match, a **preview says what happens to each row** (add, update, skip, refuse and why) before anything is saved, bad rows download as a file, *add only* or *add and update* (matched by code, then name; empty cells keep the old value), and the whole file is **one saved change and one History entry**. A blank template can be downloaded for each section.
- **Share button** on every record and report: WhatsApp, e-mail, copy, the phone's own share sheet (when the device has one) and, for reports, the picture.
- **Reports as a picture (PNG)**, the width of the chosen paper, for a chat message.
- **PDFs fit the paper**: Settings > Paper size (A4, A3, Letter, Legal). Reports, contracts and labels print on that size; the test prints real PDFs of every report type and checks their size and that nothing overflows the page.
- **Settings: fonts, colours, settings file**. English and Arabic fonts (six and seven choices, loaded from Google Fonts, or the device's own), an accent colour that is **nudged until it is readable in both themes** (the contrast is shown), published to the client site too. **Settings file**: export the settings to a small JSON file and load it on another installation (company profile, business defaults, contract wording, codes, cost categories, look, paper; **never** passwords, tokens, keys, the inbox address or the project types); loading only fills the form, nothing changes until you press Save.
- **Internal codes** for material, supplier and shipment, built from a pattern you set in Settings (`MAT-{CAT}-{N:4}` gives MAT-BRD-0012; tokens `{CAT}`, `{YY}`, `{YYYY}`, `{MM}` and exactly one `{N:n}`, counted per prefix), given automatically to every new record, shown everywhere, searched, imported and printed, never changed once given; one button gives a code to the records that have none.
- **Units linked to the BOM**: a BOM line can name a material of the inventory; it takes the material's name, code, unit (piece, sheet, metre, m², kg, litre, set) and cost, shows what is in stock, and **Take from stock** books what the line still needs out of the inventory (the project remembers what was taken, so the same quantity is never taken twice; removing a line offers to put it back; the stock and the project are saved together and the History records both).
- **WhatsApp links you can change**: the button is still made from the phone number, but a client or supplier can have another number or a WhatsApp link (business message link, group invite) typed in; the address that will open is shown under the field; other sites are refused.
- **Client ID with the SKU**: every place that shows a project shows `DS-CL-1001/DS-KIT-2610-0001` (details tab with a copy button, progress, contract, dashboard); the SKU itself keeps its format and stays unique on its own.
- **Collections** (new): each project has a **collection schedule** (Payments tab): the dates and amounts the client agreed to pay, with a *split what is left into N monthly payments* helper; money received is matched to the instalments (a deposit that no instalment covers is taken off first, the rest goes to the earliest instalment), each row says *collected / part paid / N days left / overdue by N days*. **Collections** (`#collections`, Money group): every open instalment of every client, overdue first; tiles for overdue, next 7 days, next 30 days and balances with no date; filter and search; a **WhatsApp reminder** with the amount, project reference and date, and copy. The new report **Collections: instalments to collect** prints it (PDF, Excel, picture, share). The **client sees the next payment and the list** on the money card, and the contract paper has an **Agreed instalments** table.
- **Your own cost categories**: Settings > Cost categories adds direct and indirect categories (English and Arabic names); they appear in the cost lines of every project, the expense form, imports, Finance and the reports; removing one makes it read as *Other* and keeps the amounts.
- **Contact address** on the client site: the address of Settings > Company profile now shows in the contact box of the opened project as well (it was already on the search page), under the company name and above the call, WhatsApp and e-mail buttons.
- **Tests**: new suites `s22_owner`, `s23_codes_bom`, `s24_import`, `s25_look_paper_share`, `s26_collections`, `s27_cost_cats`, `s28_audit_new` (the screens of this release are audited with the same rules as the older ones). `run.py` now holds 1,022 checks (702 at 4.4), `boot.py` 24, `tools/test_get_fonts.py` 24; the last full pass was 1,022 / 1,022.
- **Found while testing (all fixed)**: a deposit was eating the first instalment of a schedule that listed only what was still to come (the allocation now takes the deposit off first); validating an instalment replaced the objects behind the rows on screen, so edits after a refused save were lost (the rows are now cleaned in place); the second language of a category was empty in lists when only one was typed; `brand.json` gained the optional `look` field (the whitelist test knows it); changing the unit of a BOM line did not refresh its stock link; removing a BOM line that had been taken from stock left the row on screen; accent text on its own tint and the client's *Pending* / *Overdue* schedule badges were just under 4.5:1 on the dark theme; the contract's *Open Settings* link was 22 px tall on a phone (see [QUALITY.md](QUALITY.md)).
- **Not done on purpose / still open**: nothing here was tested against real WhatsApp, Google Fonts or a real phone share sheet (the sandbox has none): the links are checked for their address only; an imported `.xlsx` is read from the first sheet, formulas as their saved values; the administrator password sign-in is only as strong as the password because the login file is public (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)); Excel files with macros or password protection are refused; fonts are only downloaded in *Google Fonts* mode, the picture of a report uses the fonts of the device.

## 4.4 - Team and roles, History of every change, Finance (direct / indirect / total cost), organised Inventory table, ratings sent from the site, Troubleshooter

Data formats are compatible: 4.4 only **adds** things (see [DATA-FORMAT.md](DATA-FORMAT.md#compatibility)). A repository without the new files behaves like 4.3.

- **Team and roles** (`#team`, administrator only). Add members with a user name and a temporary password (they must choose their own at the first sign-in); five ready roles (Manager, Accountant, Production, Sales, Viewer), editable, plus your own; for each of ten areas a role is *No access / View / Edit*. **Team and Configuration (admin password, token, connection codes, key change) can never be given to a role.** Members sign in on a second tab of the lock screen; their menu, buttons, forms and report list follow their role, and a write guard refuses saving a file their role does not cover. **Pause** (reversible), **Block** (login file deleted), reset password, remove, and **Change the encryption key** (one atomic commit; the full cut-off together with replacing the GitHub token). *Honest limit:* the roles are enforced by the page, not by GitHub (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md#team-roles-and-the-history-44-what-they-really-protect)).

Who (with role), when, area, action, record and each field before / after; sealed monthly files with a hash chain (an edited or removed entry in the middle shows **Broken**); filters, months, show more; entries that could not be written wait and are retried; never blocks a save. Printable **History** report (paper, PDF, Excel).
- **Finance** (`#finance`): **direct cost, indirect cost and total operations cost** for any period (this month ... all time, custom), operating profit, overhead rate, break-even, fixed costs per month; the cost of every project with its share of the shared costs (spread by contract price, direct cost or equally; the shares always add up to the shared cost); an **expense ledger** (one-off, monthly, yearly, direct or indirect, categories, supplier) with its own form; a **monthly budget** with over/left; a printable **Costs** report (KPIs, charts, tables, Excel with native charts).

- **Ratings sent from the site itself**: the client presses **Send my rating**; the encrypted code is posted to your own free Google Apps Script inbox ([INBOX.md](INBOX.md)); the page says Sent / Sent, not confirmed / Not sent yet and keeps the rating for a retry; WhatsApp, e-mail and copy stay as backups. The admin dashboard pulls new ratings by itself (at most every 10 minutes) and with **Check for new ratings**. Settings has a new card (address, private pick-up key, key maker, test button).
- **Troubleshooter** (`#doctor`, also linked from the lock screen): checks the browser, the files of the page, fonts, GitHub (reachable, requests left, clock) and, after sign-in, the repository, public site, vault, data files, History and the signed-in person; each problem says what to do; **Copy report** holds no secret. The start-up diagnostics of `guard.js` now really name the files that did not load.
- **Design and ease of use**: new screens follow one page head (title, encryption badge, actions), a split bar for direct vs indirect, phone cards instead of wide tables, 40 px targets and 16 px fields on phones, in both languages and both themes (audited, see [QUALITY.md](QUALITY.md)); loading screens with a spinner on every new screen.
- **Tests**: new suites `s15_team`, `s16_history`, `s17_finance`, `s18_inventory_table`, `s19_inbox`, `s21_doctor`; the audit is now `s20_audit` and covers every new screen and every state of the rating box; `boot.py` also checks that the start-up diagnostics blame only the really missing file. 702 checks in `run.py`, 24 in `boot.py`, 24 in the font tool test.
- **Found while testing (all fixed)**: changing the encryption key failed with "not a fast-forward" when the History writer committed at the same moment (the writer is now paused and flushed first); the Troubleshooter's "files of this page" check was blocked by the page's own security policy (`connect-src 'self'` added; the same defect hid in the start-up diagnostics); the client rating form printed the word **null** between the comment box and the button (a null child passed to `replaceChildren`; `mount()` and the two screens that spread arrays now drop empty children); a screen-reader-only label inside the inventory table widened the whole page on phones; the History's record links, the table header buttons and the role selects were under 40 px on phones; a missing dictionary key in the inventory table that the checker could not see (the checker now looks for any text that looks like a key).

## 4.3 - Logistics, Supply chain management, Inventory management and printable reports for every section

- **Logistics** (admin menu, `#logistics`): shipments *from a supplier*, *to a client* (client and project picked from your clients) and *between places*. Each shipment has steps you can **add, edit, reorder, remove** and complete: status (waiting, in progress, done, blocked), planned date, done date and note. A new shipment starts with standard steps for its type (7, 5 or 3). The list shows progress, the current step and a one-tap **Done: <step>** button; shipments are Planned, On the way, Blocked, Completed or Cancelled and flagged **Late** past their expected arrival. Materials can be listed on a shipment and linked to the inventory; **Receive into inventory** adds them to the stock once. Prints as a one-page sheet with initials and tick columns.
- **Supply chain management** (`#suppliers`): supplier directory with category, contact person, phone, second phone, e-mail, website, location, map link, other link, address, terms, lead time and notes. Every detail is a **safe link** (call, WhatsApp with Egyptian numbers handled, mail, website, map); `javascript:`, `data:` and other non-web addresses and malformed phones and e-mails are refused. Search, category and active/inactive filters, a supplier page that lists its materials and shipments, deletion blocked while a supplier is in use (deactivate instead).
- **Inventory management system** (`#inventory`): all materials with quantity, minimum, maximum, order quantity, unit cost, supplier, place and expiry date. **An alert for every item**: out of stock, low, expired, expiring within 30 days (need attention), above maximum, no supplier, no minimum (information). Receive / Use / Stock-take with a history of the last 40 movements per material, a **to-order list grouped by supplier** with suggested quantities that already subtract what is on order, summary tiles, filters, and a number on the menu item.
- **Printable reports for every section** (Print buttons on the three screens, also in Reports): *Inventory: stock list and alerts*, *Reorder list (by supplier)*, *Stock movements*, *Supplier directory*, *Logistics: shipments*, *Shipment sheet*. Same paper, PDF and Excel (native charts) as the existing reports, English and Arabic.
- **Dashboard card** with the stock alerts, late and blocked shipments and open shipments.
- **Storage**: three sealed files under `data/private/ops/` (never published, not rewritten when the admin password changes), changed one record at a time with automatic re-apply when another device saved first.
- **Tests**: new suites `s11_supply_chain`, `s12_inventory`, `s13_logistics`, `s14_ops_reports` (real PDFs and Excel files checked), the audit moved to `s15_audit` and now covers the new screens; the destructive suite checks the data still opens after the password change. 492 checks in `run.py`.
- **Found while testing**: the menu alert number made the page scroll sideways on phones; badge contrast on the dark theme; a 36 px link; report tables breaking ids and dates across lines; a 422 console error after reloads. All fixed (see [QUALITY.md](QUALITY.md)). A test suite left a print-media emulation switched on for later suites, which made a PDF test include the menu; fixed in the test.
- **Not done on purpose**: using materials in a project's BOM does not subtract them from the inventory automatically (you record what was used); there is no bar-code scanning.
